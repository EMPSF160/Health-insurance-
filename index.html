Frequently Asked Questions

Auto Coverage Answers

What is covered under ApexShield Auto Service Roadside Assistance?
Our Roadside package covers 24/7 nationwide towing up to 100 miles, emergency fuel delivery, battery jumpstarts, flat tire changes, and vehicle lockout services with zero out-of-pocket costs.
How does Accident Forgiveness work?
After maintaining a clean driving record for 3 consecutive years with ApexShield, your policy automatically qualifies for Accident Forgiveness. If you experience an eligible accident, your premium rate will not increase.
Can I select my own repair shop after an accident?
Yes! While we maintain a certified network of over 15,000 preferred repair facilities with guaranteed repair quality, you are free to choose any licensed repair shop.
